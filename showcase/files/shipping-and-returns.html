We do not ship outside the countries listed above. Spare batteries sold on their own can only be shipped by road within the EU, because of dangerous-goods rules.

Tracking your order

You receive an e-mail with a tracking link as soon as your order ships. Bikes are delivered by appointment: the carrier calls you the day before to agree on a time slot.

Damaged or incomplete deliveries

Check your parcel when it arrives. Report any damage within 48 hours with photos of the bike and the box, and keep the packaging until the claim is settled. We then send the missing part or collect the bike free of charge.

Returns

Accessories and clothing

Unused accessories and clothing in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Return shipping is free from mainland France; from other countries it is paid by the customer.

E-bikes

You can return an e-bike within 30 days of delivery. If the bike has been ridden less than 50 km and has no damage, you get a full refund. If it shows signs of use, a restocking fee of 10% of the price is deducted.

E-bikes ridden more than 50 km cannot be returned, but they are still covered by the warranty.

To return a bike, contact us first. You can bring it to the Canal Quarter store for free, or we collect it in mainland France for €49.

What cannot be returned

  • Custom-built bikes (special paint, custom components)
  • Gift cards
  • Opened hygiene items such as helmet liners and saddle covers

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

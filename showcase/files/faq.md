# Loopline E-Bikes: Frequently asked questions

> Loopline E-Bikes is a fictional shop. This knowledge base exists to demo CiteDesk.

## Stores and opening hours

We have two locations:

- **Canal Quarter store** (sales, test rides, rentals and accessories): Tuesday to Saturday 10:00–19:00. From April to September we are also open on Sundays, 10:00–16:00. Closed on Mondays.
- **Hilltop workshop** (repairs and servicing only, no sales): Monday to Friday 8:30–18:00.

Both locations are closed on public holidays.

## What we sell

We sell e-bikes (city, cargo and e-MTB models), accessories, spare parts and cycling clothing. We do not sell e-scooters or regular (non-electric) bikes.

## Test rides

You can take a free 30-minute test ride on any model at the Canal Quarter store. Bring a photo ID. Walk-ins are welcome on weekdays; on weekends please book online, because slots fill up quickly.

## Payment options

We accept debit and credit cards, bank transfer and gift cards. Orders from €500 to €6,000 can be paid in 3 or 4 interest-free instalments through our payment partner, subject to approval at checkout.

## Choosing a frame size

Our bikes come in three frame sizes:

- S: riders from 155 to 170 cm
- M: riders from 168 to 183 cm
- L: riders from 180 to 195 cm

If you are between two sizes, book a test ride and try both.

## Delivery and assembly

Bikes are delivered 95% assembled. You only need to straighten the handlebar and fit the pedals; a tool and a video guide come in the box. Your first adjustment in store is free.

## First service

Every new bike gets a free first service after 300 km or 3 months, whichever comes first. Book it with the Hilltop workshop.

## Trade-in

We accept trade-ins of e-bikes less than 5 years old from any brand. After an inspection at the Hilltop workshop we offer store credit towards a new Loopline bike.

## Contacting us

The quickest way to reach us is this chat. You can also e-mail support@loopline.example; we reply within one working day.
